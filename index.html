What's in a score

The rating is not a Tripadvisor average. It is a single number for how much a wide ranging, experience hungry traveller would get from the thing itself.

Novelty first

How little of this you can get anywhere else. A clockmaker's workshop or a prison rodeo outranks a very good brewery tour, even when both are excellent Saturdays.

Then the whole experience

Thrill, craft, hospitality, freedom to roam, value for money, and how well the operator treats a first timer. Reviews are read for service and welcome, not just star count.

Who it's for

Young adults are the default bias, but family institutions, retiree rituals and kid first places stay in when they are genuinely world class. Party size tells you how to book it.
