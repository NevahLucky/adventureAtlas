How the radius works

Drive times are routing estimates with no traffic from downtown {{ORIGIN}} to the town or trailhead in each entry, using OpenStreetMap road data. Add 15–25% for rush hour, and check the park's own directions for the last mile.

Why some obvious places are missing

The atlas only lists things worth a deliberate trip. A chain restaurant or a generic mall doesn't make it just because it's close. If something local belongs here, it can be added.

Reading the score

0–100 for how much a curious traveller with wide tastes would get out of it: novelty, thrill, craft, hospitality, freedom and value. Sorted high to low by default.
