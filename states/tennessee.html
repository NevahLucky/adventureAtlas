How to read the score

One number, 0–100, for how much a curious traveller with wide tastes would get out of it: novelty, thrill, craft, hospitality, freedom and value, biased toward young adults, not exclusive to them.

Costs and dates

Indicative per person ranges for the activity itself, gear and standard guiding. Festivals and seasonal places list their window; always confirm the current year with the operator.

Party size

A suggestion, not a rule: “2–4” is where it sings, “NA” means it truly doesn't matter. Useful when you're assembling family versus friends.
